Peek is a client-side scheduling framework that reorders pending requests via depth-first traversal of a prefix trie to co-locate prefix-sharing requests. Integrated a shadow LRU cache model and future-queue lookahead for cross-wave cache preservation, achieving up to 11.8x throughput improvement and raising cache hit rates from 1.0% to 92.8% under heavy memory pressure. Generalizes across SGLang and vLLM with no engine modifications.
